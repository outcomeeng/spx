# Open Issues

## A decision-file target resolves to its owning node, and nothing marks that the decision was the operand

**Evidence:** the assertion of `target-resolution.md` that "a decision identifies its directly containing node or product root and selects that container's projection" is implemented by `specContextAcceptedPaths` in `src/lib/spec-tree/context-target.ts`, which accepts each ADR and PDR path with the target of its `parentId`, or the product root when it has none. The resolved result is a `SpecContextTarget` carrying only the container's path and node. Resolution collapses the decision operand and the container operand to one identity, so no field of the result, of the projection, or of the `list` and `show` outputs records that the caller named a decision file. The selection reasons `target`, `product`, and `issue` that the projection binds describe the container alone.

**Impact:** a caller that names `spx/<node>/21-example.adr.md` receives the same output as a caller that names `spx/<node>`. The output carries no mark that the named decision was not the target, so a caller cannot tell whether the decision it named was delivered because it governs the container or because it was the operand, and a typo that resolves a decision path to the wrong container is indistinguishable from a deliberate container target.

**Settlement condition:** the declarations state whether the identity of a named decision is observable in resolution and in the projection. When it is observable, `target-resolution.md` and [`spx/31-spec-domain.enabler/43-context-ingestion.enabler/32-context-manifest-schema.pdr.md`](spx/31-spec-domain.enabler/43-context-ingestion.enabler/32-context-manifest-schema.pdr.md) declare the field or selection reason that carries it, and the resolution result and the outputs carry it. When it is not, the declarations state that a decision operand and its container operand are the same target in every observable respect, and this entry is removed.
